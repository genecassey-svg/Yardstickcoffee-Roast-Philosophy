Our Roaster's Commitment

Our Roaster's Commitment

Every coffee entrusted to us represents years of work before it reaches the roastery. Farmers cultivate and harvest the cherries, producers process them with care, exporters and importers preserve their quality, and our sourcing team selects coffees that meet our standards. Roasting is our opportunity to honor that collective effort.

As roasters, our responsibility is not simply to apply heat to green coffee. Our role is to reveal each coffee's inherent character through disciplined, consistent, and intentional roasting. Every decision we make should increase the likelihood that the coffee expresses its full potential.

We Commit To

Respecting the Work of Coffee Producers

We value and recognize the knowledge, skill, and dedication required to produce exceptional coffee. Every bag of green coffee represents years of cultivation, careful harvesting, processing, and countless decisions made at origin.

Our responsibility is to preserve—not overshadow—that work. Roasting should reveal the producer's achievement rather than replace it with the roaster's preferences.

Roasting with Consistency and Intention

Consistency means producing repeatable, high-quality results from batch to batch. Customers should experience the same balance, sweetness, and character every time they enjoy the same coffee.

Intention means every roasting decision has a purpose. Charge temperature, heat application, airflow, development time, and end temperature are selected deliberately to achieve the desired sensory outcome.

Consistency builds trust.
Intention creates purpose.

Great roasting requires both.

Making Decisions Based on Sensory Evidence

Roast data provides valuable information, but numbers alone do not define quality.

Every roasting decision should ultimately be validated through sensory evaluation. We rely on cupping, aroma, sweetness, acidity, body, clarity, and balance to determine whether a roast profile successfully expresses the coffee.

Continuously Improving Our Craft

Roasting is a continuous learning process.

Every batch provides an opportunity to deepen our understanding of coffee, refine our techniques, improve consistency, and strengthen our craftsmanship.

We remain curious, open to feedback, and committed to continuous improvement.

Delivering Coffee That Reflects Its True Potential

Our objective is not to create flavor.

Our objective is to reveal what already exists within the coffee.

We strive to develop sweetness, clarity, transparency, and balance while preserving the unique characteristics that define each origin.

Success is measured not by how much influence the roaster leaves on the coffee, but by how faithfully the coffee expresses itself.

Roaster's Commitment Statement

We are Yardstick.

We believe great coffee already possesses extraordinary character. Our responsibility is to reveal—not redefine—that character through disciplined craftsmanship, thoughtful decision-making, and consistent execution.

We roast with respect for every producer, with intention behind every decision, and with commitment to continuous improvement.

Every coffee we roast should honestly reflect its origin, celebrate the work of those who produced it, and deliver an exceptional experience in every cup.

Through this commitment, we continue to raise the standard of what great coffee can be.
